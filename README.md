# 👋 Hey, I'm Rajat

## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm Rajat, a final-year student and developer who enjoys building things and understanding how they work under the hood.

I started with frontend development and have been gradually moving toward full-stack development, backend systems, cloud, and AI. I learn best by building real projects, experimenting with ideas, and figuring out problems along the way.

Right now, I'm working with **React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, and Prisma**, while exploring **system design, cloud, DevOps, and agentic AI**.

I believe in learning by building, keeping things simple, and understanding the architecture behind the code rather than just making things work.

---

## 🔨 What I'm Building

### 📝 DevNotes
A full-stack note-taking application built with **Next.js, PostgreSQL, and Prisma**, with AI-powered interaction with personal notes using **RAG**.

### 🎥 Summora
A video transcription and summarization application that converts video content into **text transcripts and concise summaries**, making long-form video content easier to understand and revisit.

### ✅ Habit Tracker
A full-stack habit tracking application focused on tracking consistency, visualizing progress, and understanding how a frontend application connects to a real backend system.

### 🎫 Support CRM
A customer support and ticket management application built around a **REST API, backend architecture, and database-driven workflows**.
